SETTINGS & USER GUIDE

🧙 Hero & XP

You can change your Hero emoji, Hero name, and class title from the Headquarters banner. Use Reset Hero XP to bring your total Hero XP back to zero and restart from Level 1.

  • Level 1–4: Novice Adventurer
  • Level 5–9: Apprentice Hero
  • Level 10–19: Seasoned Warrior
  • Level 20–34: Elite Champion
  • Level 35–49: Legendary Hero
  • Level 50+: Mythic Overlord

🏠 Headquarters

Headquarters shows today’s priorities, this week’s pending/overdue counts, and separate all-weeks Pending Tasks and Overdue Tasks sections. The week starts every Monday and ends every Sunday.

✅ Tasks

  • Add tasks with start date, start time, end time, priority, role, recurrence, and notes.
  • Pending means the task is not complete and its scheduled end time has not passed.
  • Overdue means the task is not complete and its scheduled date/time has passed.

⏱️ Timeline

The Timeline places tasks based on the selected day and the task start/end time. Use the left and right arrows to move between days.

🎭 Roles

Roles help group your tasks by life area, such as Career, Fitness, Family, Learning, Finance, or Health. You can add/edit roles from the Roles screen or from the task form.

🔁 Recurrence

Daily, weekly, monthly, hourly, and custom recurrence are supported. Weekly recurrence can repeat on selected weekdays from Monday to Sunday. Custom recurrence can repeat by interval or by selected weekdays.

⚙ Simple Mode

Simple Mode hides XP, levels, streaks, and gamified visuals. Your tasks, roles, timeline, journal, and settings remain available.

☁️ Sync

Tasks save locally first and then sync to Firebase when permissions and internet are available. Use the same login email on laptop and mobile for cross-device sync.

📅 Google Calendar Sync

Pull events from your Google Calendar as tasks. Each event is pulled only once — duplicates are automatically skipped on future syncs.
